# CHAKE Website

This context captures product and content language for the CHAKE public website. It exists so contributors use the same terms when planning site structure, program presentation, and donor-facing storytelling.

## Language

**Visual content architecture**:
The page-to-image and program-to-image structure that determines which imagery belongs with each CHAKE page, program area, or gallery.
_Avoid_: architecture from the pictures, picture architecture, image decoration

## Relationships

- A **Visual content architecture** maps CHAKE pages to relevant imagery from organization, program, project, training, and support contexts.
- A **Visual content architecture** keeps imagery tied to content meaning rather than using images as decoration.

## Example Dialogue

> **Dev:** "Should the services page use any strong image from the parent site?"
> **Domain expert:** "No, follow the **Visual content architecture**: health images belong with health services, training images belong with skills or enrollment, and staff images belong with about or organization galleries."

## Flagged Ambiguities

- "architecture from the pictures" was ambiguous; resolved term is **Visual content architecture**, meaning page-to-image and program-to-image structure rather than literal building architecture.
